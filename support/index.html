Skip to Simmer Support

Email support@rpowered.net for help with Skip to Simmer.

Turn on the Safari extension

Open Skip to Simmer once. On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Skip to Simmer. On older iOS versions, Safari appears directly in Settings. On Mac, go to Safari → Settings → Extensions. Turn on Skip to Simmer and allow it on the recipe site.

On supported recipe pages, tap + Skip to Simmer to open the card. Tap Save to add the recipe to the app on this device.

Find an archived recipe

Open Archive from the library toolbar. Select a recipe and tap Unarchive recipe to return it to the library.

Send a support request

Include your device model, OS version, what you expected, and what happened. If a particular page is affected, include its address if you’re comfortable sharing it.
